Paid project access.

CAD, print files and assembly manuals are not offered as free downloads. The DETENT D25 personal-use digital package costs US$19 as a one-time purchase. It includes the identified D25 files and documentation. No physical product is supplied. Checkout and paid file delivery are handled by a separate digital-download platform. This website does not collect payment details or host the paid project files.

Personal-use licence

You may download, print, assemble and modify the supplied original DETENT files for your own personal use. This licence does not allow selling physical prints, redistributing or sharing the digital files, or selling digital copies or derivatives. Third-party notices and rights remain applicable. The purchase covers the D25 package; future releases are not promised as part of this purchase.

Commercial printing

The initial commercial offer is US$29 for 12 months for one named maker or business. Selling physical prints requires a written commercial grant specifying its scope, covered revision and term. Buying or requesting project access alone does not grant commercial manufacturing rights. Digital-file resale is not included.

Requests and activation

Commercial licence requests use the public GitHub form with only a public maker name and intended use. Do not post private or payment details. A request does not activate a commercial licence. Personal-use files are delivered by the checkout provider after a successful purchase.

Development status

D25 remains a development prototype with geometry checks, not a validated load rating. Paid access does not certify safety, print strength or suitability for a particular laptop or desk.

Previous distributions

This change stops new free distribution through this project. It does not revoke licences already granted for previously distributed copies.

Privacy

No analytics or payment processing. Color and language preferences are stored in your browser. GitHub hosts the site and public access requests.
